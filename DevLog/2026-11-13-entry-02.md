## What I did

*Project Void: Breach* is a 3D mobile game in which the player controls a black hole that has escaped a containment laboratory. My part is the military helicopter boss deployed in the final arena. The plan for this period was the boss finite state machine and the lock-on mechanism; both are now working, together with the attack systems built on top of them.

- **State machine** — four states (Idle, NormalAttack, BerserkMode, Defeated). Berserk starts at 50 % health with a faster fire rate, and Defeated disables the colliders, rolls the aircraft into a dive and destroys it.
- **Machine-gun strafe** — two muzzles fire a burst that is locked to the player's position at the moment it starts, so it can be dodged. A translucent red rectangle is projected on the ground for 0.7 s as a warning, then the impact point walks from the ground up to the player.
- **Tracers** — each shot draws a short six-unit segment that travels to the impact point and disappears. The gaps between shots are what make it read as bullets rather than a laser.
- **Homing missile** — a 0.25 s arming delay so it cannot detonate inside the aircraft, an explosion on timeout instead of silently vanishing, and a prefab refill once the four modelled missiles are used up.
- **Damage and health** — damage goes through an `IDamageable` interface instead of `SendMessage`, and the boss raises health and defeated events so a health bar can subscribe later.

## Problems faced

| Problem | Cause and fix |
|---|---|
| Missiles flew through the player and never exploded. | The missile prefab already had a non-trigger collider and a kinematic rigidbody, and the player is kinematic too, so `OnTriggerEnter` could never fire. Fixed by forcing the collider to be a trigger at launch and adding the arming delay. |
| The boss stopped attacking after its first burst. | `CompareTag("Player")` threw because the tag was not defined in the project; the exception killed the coroutine before the attacking flag was reset. Fixed by defining the tag, switching to `IDamageable`, and resetting the flag in a `finally` block. |
| The attack could not be dodged at all. | Every shot re-aimed at the player's live position with an instant raycast. Fixed by locking the aim line at the start of the burst and previewing it on the ground. |
